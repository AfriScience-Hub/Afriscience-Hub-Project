## 1.1 Listing Form (Dashboard)

### Basic Profile Information
* **Profile Image:** Upload facial image of scientist/technologist *(Images only)*
* **Background Image:** Upload background image *(Images only)*
* **Name:** Enter full name
* **Country:** Select Country *(Global dropdown)*
* **State / Region:** Select State/Region *(Global dropdown)*
* **Short Bio / Description:** Enter Bio *(Info: Tell us more about yourself, qualifications, skills, career, etc. Max 1000 words)*

### Professional Details
* **Field:** Select Field *(Max 1 selection: Hybrid, Medical, Engineering, Pharmaceutical, Computer & ICT, Veterinary, Agriculture, Food & Nutrition, Mathematics & Statistics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Environmental, Industrial & Manufacturing, Energy & Power, Waste & Recycling, Astronomy & Space)*
* **Profession:** Select Profession *(Max 4 selections; entries made via admin dashboard. If "Other" is selected, user must specify)*
* **Degree:** Select Degree *(Max 1 selection: Field Expert | No Degree, Associate, Bachelors, Diplomas, Masters, Doctoral, Others. Upload Degree Certificate to verify academic qualification)*
* **Services:** Select Services *(Multiple selections allowed: Architecture & Framework Design, Building & Construction, Consultancy, Content Creation, Education & Training, Entrepreneurship & Business, Fabrication & Installation, Fieldwork, Job Applicant, Lab Analysis & Equipment Expert, Modeling & Simulation, Project Management, Repairs & Maintenance, Research & Development, Writing & Documentations)*
* **Service Cost:** Input Service 1 (Service Name, Description, Cost Range, Currency). Automatically lists inputs for all selected services.
* **Other Charges:** Input Charge 1 (Charge Name, Description, Cost, Currency). Multiple entries allowed.
* **Scopes:** Enter Scopes *(List expertise areas: tests, topics, techniques, tools, instrumentation, etc. Multiple entries allowed)*
* **Engagement Policies:** Enter Policies *(Guidelines for clients when requesting services. Multiple entries allowed)*
* **Certifications & Degrees:** File 1 (Certificate/Degree Name, Issued By, Year, Upload Document). Multiple entries allowed.
* **Major Achievements & Awards:** Achievement 1 (Achievement/Award Name, Issued By, Year, Upload Document). Multiple entries allowed.
* **Media Gallery:** Upload media files of services/expertise *(Max 10 files, pictures or videos)*
* **Contact Information:** Phone Number, E-mail, Website (optional), Social Handles (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook)
