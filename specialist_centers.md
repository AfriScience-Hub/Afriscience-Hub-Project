## 1.1 Listing Form (Dashboard)

### Your Information (Auto-populated by platform)
* Full Name
* Phone Number
* E-mail Address
* ID Tag

### Center Identity
* **Profile Image:** Upload Profile Image
* **Background Image:** Upload Background Image
* **Center Name:** Enter establishment name
* **Center Motto:** Enter motto or tagline
* **Center Address:** Enter establishment address
* **Country:** Select Country *(Global dropdown)*
* **State / Region:** Select State/Region *(Global dropdown)*
* **Phone Number:** Enter establishment phone number
* **E-mail:** Enter establishment email address
* **Website:** Enter establishment website (optional)
* **Social Handles:** LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, Facebook *(Provide at least one)*
* **Center Description:** Detailed description *(Max 1000 words)*

### Center Information
* **Fields:** Select Field *(Max 1 selection: Hybrid, Medical, Engineering, Pharmaceutical, Computer & ICT, Veterinary, Agriculture, Food & Nutrition, Mathematics & Statistics, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Environmental, Industrial & Manufacturing, Energy & Power, Waste & Recycling, Astronomy & Space)*
* **Categories:** Select Categories *(Max 4 selections; entries made via admin dashboard. If "Other" is selected, user must specify)*
* **Ownership:** Select Ownership *(Max 1 selection: Private, Government | Public, Academic, Mission, Corporate, Inter-Government, NGO | Charity, Other. Specify if "Other")*
* **Services:** Select Services *(Multiple selections allowed; entries made via admin dashboard)*
* **Service Fee:** Input Service 1 (Service Name, Description, Cost Range, Currency). Auto-lists all selected services.
* **Other Fees:** Input Charge 1 (Charge Name, Description, Cost, Currency). Multiple entries allowed.
* **Scopes:** Enter Scopes *(List specific areas of expertise: tests, techniques, tools, instrumentation, etc. Multiple entries allowed)*
* **Engagement Policies:** Enter Policies *(Important guidelines for client requests. Multiple entries allowed)*
* **Licenses & Certifications:** File 1 (License/Certificate Name, Issued By, Year, Upload Document). Multiple entries allowed.
* **Achievements & Honorary Awards:** Achievement 1 (Achievement/Award Name, Issued By, Year, Upload Document). Multiple entries allowed.
* **Media Gallery:** Upload media into designated albums (Front View, Compound/Premise, Hallways/Lobbies, Operations Space, Instruments, Laboratories, Libraries, Offices, Others). Max 10 files per album.
